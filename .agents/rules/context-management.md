# Context management

Classify context as authoritative source, verified source-bound fact, historical material, or speculation. A reusable fact records the source revision, exact source location, verifier role, verification time, and invalidation condition.

Keep task context small. Do not copy whole plans or conversations into task packets. Reverify facts when their source changes or when a safety or scientific decision depends on them.

Task packets and handoffs are operational aids. They never become a second scientific source of truth.
