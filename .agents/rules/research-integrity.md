# Research integrity

Every scientific claim must trace to current source, exact configuration, input identity, environment identity, immutable evidence, and the governing acceptance contract. Preserve failed, negative, incomplete, censored, and unsupported outcomes.

Do not use frozen test data for model selection. Do not infer geometric LOS from profile labels. Do not treat a finite ray budget as ground truth. Keep source permission, scientific validity, and public-release eligibility as separate dispositions.
